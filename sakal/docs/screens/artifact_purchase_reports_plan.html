Purchase Module · Reporting Blueprint

Thirteen reports, one purchase flow

Purchase has zero reports today. This maps every stage of Order → Receipt → Bill → Return, plus cross-module analysis against Inventory, to a specific report — cross-checked against what Finance already covers (supplier payable ageing) so nothing here duplicates it, and what the schema genuinely can't support yet, called out rather than quietly worked around.

Schema change Report 11 (Vendor On-Time Delivery) needs one new nullable column — rid_purchase_order_lines.expected_delivery_date — set at the LINE level, not the header, since different items on one order commonly promise different arrival dates. Every other report below runs on the schema exactly as it stands today.

07

Purchase Charges Register

Tabular

Freight/handling charges booked at receipt time, across all GRNs in the period.

Filters

Date range — required Supplier (goods supplier)LocationCharge Type
Column
GRN No · Date · Supplier · Charge Type · Nature (Add/Deduct) · Amount · GL Account · Tax
Genuine schema gap, shown rather than hidden: no document in Purchase today ever clears a charge against its own supplier (freight is usually a different vendor than the goods supplier). Every row here stays open indefinitely — this report exists so that exposure is at least visible. A real fix would be a future "Charge Bill" concept; out of scope for this pass.

Billing stage

What we've actually been invoiced for
05

Purchase Invoice (Bill) Register

Grouped

A Purchase Bill has no line items of its own — it IS a set of received GRNs. So the group row here is the Bill, and its "detail rows" are whole GRN documents, not items — an honest reflection of how this document actually works, not a workaround.

Filters

Date range — required SupplierStatus
Column
GroupInvoice No · Date · Supplier Invoice No/Date · Supplier · Taxable Amount · Tax Amount · Invoice Total · Exchange Diff · Status
DetailGRN No · GRN Date · GRN Grand Total
Verify before build: the Bill header wasn't confirmed to carry its own location_id (a bill can consolidate GRNs from different locations). If it doesn't, Location filtering here narrows by "has at least one linked GRN at that location" instead of a direct column filter.
13

Purchase Tax Summary

Grouped

Input VAT actually invoiced by suppliers, grouped by supplier — a compliance-oriented total for VAT/GST return filing.

Filters

Date range — required SupplierStatus (Approved by default)
Column
GroupSupplier · Total Taxable Amount · Total Tax Amount · Bill Count
DetailInvoice No · Date · Supplier Invoice No/Date · Taxable Amount · Tax Amount · Invoice Total
Known limitation, stated plainly: tax is reported at the whole-bill level, not broken down by individual tax rate — Purchase Bill doesn't apportion Input VAT the way Sales Invoice apportions output tax. Good enough for a total-tax-paid figure; a rate-by-rate breakdown is a future enhancement, not assumed here.
